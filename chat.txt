## The Secret Sauce: Opinion Extraction

Don't store entire articles.

Store extracted facts.

Example:

YouTube review says:

  "The Garmin R50 is incredibly portable but struggles in direct sunlight."

Store:

JSON

{
  "product": "Garmin R50",
  "pro": "portable",
  "con": "poor sunlight performance",
  "sentiment": 0.72
}

Do this thousands of times.

Now MHG can answer:

  "Show me launch monitors praised for portability."

without relying on AI at runtime.
